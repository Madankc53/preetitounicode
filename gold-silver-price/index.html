Gold and silver units in Nepal

Gold and silver in Nepal are priced per tola. One tola is 11.6638038 grams (exactly 3/8 of a troy ounce). So 10 grams is about 0.857 tola, and one troy ounce is 8/3 tola, about 2.667 tola.

The calculator shows metal value only. A jeweller's bill usually adds making charges (मजुरी), wastage and taxes, and the price depends on purity. Always check the final price in writing before you buy or sell.

Where the rate comes from

The daily gold and silver rate is the one announced by the Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers' Association (FENEGOSIDA). This page copies it once or twice a day and shows the date. If the update fails, the page says so and shows a warning instead of an old number as if it were current.

Frequently asked questions

What is the weight of 1 tola in grams?

One tola is 11.6638038 grams. Ten grams is therefore about 0.857 tola.

Who sets the gold and silver price in Nepal?

The rate shown here is the one announced by FENEGOSIDA, the federation of gold and silver dealers. Jewellers may charge extra for making, wastage and taxes.

Why does the page say the rate is not available?

The rate is copied automatically from the source. If the source could not be read or the number looked wrong, the page shows no rate rather than a wrong one. You can still use the calculator with a price you enter.

Is the 10 gram price official?

No. It is calculated from the price per tola using 11.6638038 grams per tola, so it can differ slightly from a price quoted per 10 grams elsewhere.

Does the calculator include making charges and VAT?

No. It gives the value of the metal at the price you enter. Ask the jeweller for the full bill.
