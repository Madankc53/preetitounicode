Spotted a wrong date, a broken page, or have an idea for a tool? Here is how to reach us.

Report a mistake or suggest a tool

The fastest way to reach us is to open an issue on GitHub. It creates a public record, so we can fix the problem and you can see when it is done. A free GitHub account is needed.

Open an issue on GitHub

Please include

  • The page address (URL) and which tool you were using.
  • What you entered, what you expected, and what you got instead. A BS date example is ideal.
  • For a date or holiday correction, a link to the official source (NPNS, MoHA or a gazette notice).
  • Your device and browser, if the page looks broken.

Any other enquiry

For anything that is not a bug report, such as partnerships, advertising or press, use the contact page on our main site: thenepal.io/contact-us.
