Why a phone photo is so big

A modern phone camera takes pictures with many millions of pixels, so one photo is often 2 to 6 MB. An online form that allows only 100 KB is asking for a file roughly 20 to 60 times smaller. Two things make a file smaller: lower image quality (more compression) and fewer pixels (a smaller picture). The compressor adjusts both automatically until the file fits.

How to reduce a photo to 50 KB or 100 KB, step by step

  1. Open the Image CompressorGo to the Image Compressor in your phone browser.
  2. Add your photoTap Choose images to pick from your gallery, or Take photo to use the camera.
  3. Choose the size limitTap 20, 50, 100, 200 or 500 KB, 1 MB, or type your own number.
  4. Check the resultThe new size appears beside the old one. Tap Compare and drag the slider to see before and after.
  5. Download and uploadTap Download and attach the new file to your form.

Private by design. The photo is compressed inside your browser. It is not sent to any server, so it is safe to use for ID photos, certificates and signatures.

What if the photo is still too big or looks bad?

  • Choose a larger limit if the form allows it. A 100 KB photo is much sharper than a 20 KB one.
  • Keep “Reduce dimensions if needed” on. Very small limits are only possible with a smaller picture. Turn it off only if the form demands the original pixel size.
  • Need an exact pixel size too? Use the Photo & Signature Resizer, which sets width, height and maximum KB together.
  • Crop first. A tight crop around the face or document leaves fewer pixels to compress, so quality stays higher at the same size.
  • Use good light. Noisy, dark photos compress badly and end up blurrier at the same file size.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Compressing the same file again and again. Always start from the original photo, because each pass loses detail.
  • Renaming a PNG to .jpg. Convert it properly with the JPG Converter.
  • Submitting without opening the result. Look at it once to make sure a face or text is still clear.
  • Taking a photo of a document at a slant or in shadow. Retake it flat and well lit before compressing.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reduce a photo to 100 KB on my phone?

Open the Image Compressor in your phone browser, add the photo, tap 100 KB and download the result. It takes a few seconds and needs no app.

How do I reduce a photo to 50 KB?

Do the same but tap 50 KB. If the picture looks too soft, try 100 KB or crop the photo first.

Is it safe to compress my ID or certificate photo here?

Yes. The tool works inside your browser and does not upload the file, so the image stays on your device.

Why is my compressed photo smaller in pixels?

To reach a very small limit the tool may shrink the picture as well as the quality. Turn off the reduce-dimensions option if the form needs the original pixel size.

Can I compress many photos at once?

Yes. Add several photos, they are all reduced to the same limit, and you can download them together as a ZIP.

My iPhone photo will not open in the tool. What do I do?

Some iPhones save photos as HEIC. Change Settings, Camera, Formats to Most Compatible so new photos save as JPG, or share the photo as JPG before adding it.
