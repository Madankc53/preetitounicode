Updated 26 November 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer: open Split PDF, add your PDF, choose Remove these pages or Keep only these pages, type the page numbers and tap Split PDF. Nothing is uploaded.

When do you need to remove or extract PDF pages?

A scan may contain a blank page, a wrong page or pages the form does not ask for. You may also need to send only one page of a long file. Removing or extracting pages gives you a new, shorter PDF and leaves the original unchanged.

How to remove pages from a PDF, step by step

  1. Open Split PDFGo to Split PDF and choose your PDF. The tool shows the number of pages.
  2. Choose what to doPick Keep only these pages, Remove these pages or Save every page separately.
  3. Type the page numbersUse numbers and ranges, for example 1-3, 5. Page 1 is the first page of the file.
  4. Split and downloadTap Split PDF, then download the new PDF or the ZIP.

Private by design. Your browser makes the new file. The PDF is not sent to any server.

Which option should I choose?

OptionWhat you getUse it when
Keep only these pagesOne PDF with just the pages you listYou need page 4, or pages 2-3, from a long file
Remove these pagesOne PDF without the pages you listA blank or wrong page must go
Save every page separatelyA ZIP with one PDF per pageEach page must be uploaded on its own

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Counting pages wrongly. Use the page numbers shown in your PDF viewer, starting at 1.
  • Choosing Keep when you meant Remove. Keep makes a file with only the listed pages.
  • Trying a password-protected PDF. Remove the password first.
  • Not checking the result. Open the new PDF and look at every page before you upload it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove a page from a PDF on my phone?

Open Split PDF, add the PDF, choose Remove these pages, type the page number and tap Split PDF. Then download the new PDF.

How do I extract one page from a PDF?

Choose Keep only these pages and type the page number, for example 4.

Can I save every page as a separate file?

Yes. Choose Save every page separately to get a ZIP with one PDF per page, up to 300 pages.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The new file is made in your browser, so the PDF stays on your device.

Does it change my original PDF?

No. The original file stays as it is, and you get a new file.

Can I do this with a password-protected PDF?

Not directly. Remove the password first, then add the file.
