How to Make a PDF From Photos on Your Phone

Updated 24 November 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer: open JPG to PDF, add your photos, put them in order, choose A4 and tap Create PDF. Pick Small quality if the form has a size limit. It runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Before you start: take clean photos

A PDF is only as clear as the photos inside it. A few seconds of care at this stage makes a neat document:

  • Put the paper on a dark, plain surface and use daylight or a lamp from the side to avoid your own shadow.
  • Hold the phone straight above the page so the edges are square, and let the whole page fit with a small border.
  • Take one photo per page, in reading order, so they land in the gallery in the right sequence.

How to make a PDF from photos, step by step

  1. Open JPG to PDFGo to JPG to PDF and tap Choose files. Select all the photos at once.
  2. Put the pages in orderUse the arrow buttons to move pages, Rotate for sideways pictures and Remove for extras.
  3. Choose page size, margin and qualityPick A4 for most forms, a Small margin, and Balanced quality, or Small for tight limits.
  4. Create and downloadTap Create PDF. The page count and file size appear, then tap Download PDF.

Private by design. The PDF is built inside your browser. Your photos are not uploaded, which matters for citizenship, passport, bank and exam documents.

How to make the PDF smaller

Many portals limit PDFs to 1 MB or 2 MB. If yours is too big:

  • Choose Small quality before you create the PDF.
  • Remove pages the form does not need.
  • Make sure the photos are not much larger than necessary. You can first shrink each one with the Image Compressor.
  • Split the document into two PDFs if the form accepts more than one file.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Pages in the wrong order. Check the numbered list before you press Create PDF.
  • Sideways pages. Use Rotate, then look at the thumbnail.
  • Cropped edges on important text. Retake the photo with a small border all round.
  • A very large PDF from full-quality photos. Use Small quality when a limit applies.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a PDF from photos on my phone?

Open JPG to PDF in your phone browser, add the photos, arrange them, choose A4 and tap Create PDF. Then download the file.

Can I put many photos in one PDF?

Yes. Each photo becomes one page, in the order shown in the list.

How do I make the PDF under 1 MB?

Choose Small quality, remove unneeded pages, and shrink very large photos first with the Image Compressor.

Will the PDF have a watermark?

No. Nothing is added to your file.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. The PDF is created inside your browser and the photos stay on your device.

Can I rotate a sideways photo?

Yes. Tap Rotate on that page and check the thumbnail before you create the PDF.
