How to Merge PDF Files Into One on Your Phone

Updated 26 November 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer: open Merge PDF, add your PDF files, put them in the right order with the arrows, then tap Merge PDFs and Download PDF. Nothing is uploaded.

When do you need to merge PDFs?

Many job, admission and bank forms accept only one PDF but ask for several documents, such as a citizenship certificate, a transcript and a photo page. Merging puts them in a single file in the order you choose.

How to merge PDF files, step by step

  1. Open Merge PDFGo to Merge PDF and tap Choose files. You can select several PDFs at once.
  2. Put the files in orderUse the up and down arrows. The merged PDF follows the order on screen.
  3. Choose pages if neededTo use only some pages of a file, type them in its Pages box, for example 1-3, 5.
  4. Merge and downloadTap Merge PDFs, then Download PDF.

Private by design. Your browser combines the files. They are not sent to any server.

How do I keep the merged file small?

The merged file is about as large as the originals added together. If the form has an upload limit, make the parts smaller first.

  • Shrink photos with the Image Compressor before you make PDFs.
  • Make each scan a PDF with JPG to PDF and choose the Small quality.
  • Then merge the small PDFs here.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Adding a password-protected PDF. Remove the password first, because locked files cannot be merged.
  • Wrong order. Check the numbers beside each file name before you merge.
  • Including pages you do not need. Type the pages you want in the Pages box.
  • Not opening the result. Scroll through the merged PDF once before you upload it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I merge PDF files on my phone?

Open Merge PDF in your phone browser, add the PDFs, put them in order and tap Merge PDFs. Then download the new PDF.

Can I merge only some pages of a file?

Yes. Type the page numbers in the Pages box under that file, for example 1-3, 5. Leave it empty to use every page.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The files are combined in your browser, so they stay on your device.

Why is the merged PDF so large?

It is about the sum of the original files. Make the images smaller before creating the PDFs, then merge them.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Not directly. Remove the password first, then add the file.

Does merging reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied as they are, so text stays sharp.
