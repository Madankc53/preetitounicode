How to Convert a PDF to JPG on Your Phone or Computer

Updated 24 November 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer: open PDF to JPG, add your PDF, choose the quality and the pages you want, then tap Convert. Download each page as a JPG, or all of them as a ZIP. Nothing is uploaded.

When do you need a PDF as images?

Some portals accept only image files, not PDFs. You may also want to share one page in a chat app, put a page in a presentation, or edit it in a photo app. Converting a PDF to JPG gives you one picture for each page.

How to convert a PDF to JPG, step by step

  1. Open PDF to JPGGo to PDF to JPG and tap Choose file.
  2. Choose quality and formatPick Standard for most uses, High for small print, and JPG or PNG.
  3. Pick the pagesLeave Pages empty for all, or type something like 1-3, 5 for only those pages.
  4. Convert and downloadTap Convert. Download each page, or tap Download all (ZIP). If the PDF has a password, the tool asks for it.

Private by design. Your browser opens and draws the PDF. The file is not sent to any server.

Which quality should I choose?

QualityResolutionUse it for
Small96 dpiQuick previews and chat sharing
Standard150 dpiMost forms, screens and email
High220 dpiSmall print, zooming in, printing small

Higher quality means a larger file. If the image must be under a size limit, convert it and then shrink it with the Image Compressor.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Converting a huge PDF on an old phone. Pick only the pages you need, or use the Small quality.
  • Forgetting the password. A protected PDF cannot be opened without it.
  • Expecting editable text. The result is a picture of the page, not text you can edit.
  • Choosing PNG for photos. PNG files are larger, so JPG is usually better for scans and photos.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a PDF to JPG on my phone?

Open PDF to JPG in your phone browser, add the PDF, choose the quality and tap Convert. Then download each page or all pages as a ZIP.

Can I convert only some pages?

Yes. Type the page numbers in the Pages box, for example 2-4 or 1, 3. Leave it empty for all pages.

Can it open a password-protected PDF?

Yes, if you know the password. The tool asks for it and then converts the pages.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion happens inside your browser, so the file stays on your device.

Why is the JPG so large?

High quality and big pages create bigger images. Choose Standard or Small, or shrink the result with the Image Compressor.

Is there a page limit?

The tool converts up to 100 pages at a time to keep your browser responsive.
