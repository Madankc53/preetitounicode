About the Kuwaiti dinar and the Nepali rupee

Kuwait also hosts Nepali workers. The Kuwaiti dinar is one of the highest-valued currency units in the world, so a small number of dinars converts to a large number of rupees. The dinar's value is managed against a basket of foreign currencies.

The rate on this page is a mid-market reference rate. It is useful for planning, comparing offers and checking a quote, but it is not a price you can trade at.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert KWD to NPR?

Type the amount in the box at the top of the page. The converter multiplies it by the latest KWD to NPR rate and shows the result in Nepali rupees. Use the Swap button to convert NPR to KWD instead.

Is this the rate I will get from a bank or money transfer company?

Not exactly. This page shows a mid-market reference rate from a public rates feed. Banks, exchange counters and money transfer companies use their own buy and sell rates and may add fees, so the amount you receive can be lower. Always confirm the rate and fees with your provider before you send or exchange money.

How often does the rate update?

The rates feed is refreshed about once a day. The date of the rate is shown under the result, so you can see which day's rate you are looking at.

Why does the NPR rate against the Kuwaiti dinar change?

Nepal's rupee is pegged to the Indian rupee at 1.60 NPR for 1 INR. That means the rupee's value against other currencies follows the Indian rupee's value, and it moves when the Indian rupee moves.
