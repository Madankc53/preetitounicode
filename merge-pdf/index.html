    How to merge PDF files

    1. Choose the PDF files you want to combine. You can pick several at once.
    2. Use the arrows to put the files in the right order. If you only need some pages of a file, type them in the Pages box, for example 1-3, 5.
    3. Press Merge PDFs, then Download PDF.

    The files are combined by your browser, so they are never sent to a server. That keeps documents such as citizenship certificates, transcripts and bank statements on your own device.

    When you need one PDF

    • A job or admission form that accepts a single PDF but asks for several documents.
    • Scans of both sides of a certificate that are saved as separate files.
    • A report that was exported in parts.

    Keep the size small. The merged file is about as large as the originals added together. If a form has an upload limit, shrink your photos first with the image compressor, then make each scan a PDF with JPG to PDF, and merge the results here. Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked before they can be merged.

    To take pages out of a PDF, use Split PDF.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this PDF merger free?

    Yes. It is free, needs no account and adds no watermark.

    Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

    No. The files are combined in your browser on your own device, so they are not uploaded anywhere.

    Can I merge only some pages of a file?

    Yes. Type the pages in the box under the file, for example 1-3, 5. Leave the box empty to use every page.

    Can I change the order of the files?

    Yes. Use the up and down arrows next to each file. The merged PDF follows the order shown on screen.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Not directly. Remove the password first, then add the file. Files that cannot be read are skipped and listed.

    Does merging reduce the quality?

    No. Pages are copied as they are, so text stays sharp and images are not recompressed.
