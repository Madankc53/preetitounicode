    How to convert JPG to PDF

    1. Choose your JPG, PNG or WebP images. You can pick several at once, or add more later.
    2. Use the arrows to put pages in order, rotate any that are sideways, and remove those you do not need.
    3. Pick the page size, margin and quality, then press Create PDF and download the file.

    Everything runs inside your browser. Your photos are not uploaded to any server, which matters for citizenship certificates, passports, bank KYC and other private documents.

    Making a PDF small enough for a form

    Many Nepali online forms, job applications and university portals set a file-size limit. Choose Small quality to shrink the PDF. The final size is shown after conversion; if it is still too large, remove a page or convert fewer images at a time.

    Check the result. Open the PDF and make sure text on documents is still readable before you submit it. Use High quality for documents with small print.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this JPG to PDF converter free?

    Yes. It is free, with no sign-up and no watermark.

    Are my images uploaded anywhere?

    No. The conversion happens in your browser on your own device. The images never leave it.

    Can I combine many JPG images into one PDF?

    Yes. Add as many images as you like, reorder them with the arrows, and each one becomes a page in a single PDF.

    How do I make the PDF smaller?

    Choose Small quality. This reduces the image resolution and compression quality, which usually cuts the file size a lot.

    Does it work on a phone?

    Yes. On Android and iPhone, tap Choose images and pick photos from your gallery or camera.

    Can I convert PNG or WebP as well?

    Yes. PNG and WebP images are accepted and placed into the PDF as JPEG pages. Transparent areas become white.
