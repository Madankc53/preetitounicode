    How to reduce an image to 50 KB or 100 KB

    1. Add your photos by choosing them, dragging them in, taking a photo, or pasting.
    2. Pick the size limit the form asks for, or enter your own in KB. The result updates by itself.
    3. Check the before and after comparison, then download each file or all of them as a ZIP.

    The tool lowers the quality until the file fits. If quality alone is not enough and Reduce dimensions if needed is on, it also makes the picture a little smaller, so a very small limit such as 20 KB gives a smaller image.

    When you need a small image

    Online forms often limit uploads: job applications, exam and university forms, passport-size photos, signatures and scanned documents. Check the exact size and format on the form, then compress to just under that limit. Need exact pixel dimensions as well? Use the photo and signature resizer.

    Check the result. Look at the compressed image before you submit it. Very small limits reduce detail, so a signature or ID photo should still be clear and readable. Turn off Reduce dimensions if the form needs the original pixel size.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I reduce a photo to 100 KB?

    Add the photo, choose 100 KB and wait a moment. The tool finds the best quality that fits and shows the new size, with a before and after comparison.

    Is my photo uploaded to a server?

    No. Compression happens in your browser. The photo stays on your device.

    Why is my compressed image smaller in pixels?

    To reach a very small limit the tool may reduce the dimensions as well as the quality. Turn off Reduce dimensions if you must keep the original size, or choose a larger limit.

    Can I compress many images at once?

    Yes. Add several. Each is compressed to the same limit and you can download them one by one or as a ZIP.

    Which format should I choose?

    JPG works everywhere and suits most forms. WebP gives smaller files but is not accepted by every website.

    Does it work on a phone?

    Yes. You can choose from the gallery or take a photo with the camera.
