Private by design

These tools run inside your browser. Your photos, scans and PDFs are not uploaded to any server, so documents such as citizenship certificates, passport photos and bank papers stay on your device. Nothing is added to your files, and there is no watermark or sign-up.

Tips for online forms

  • Read the notice for the exact size, KB limit, format and background colour. Forms differ, and the sizes in these tools are examples.
  • Resize first, then compress. The resizer can set pixels and a maximum KB together.
  • Open the final file and check that text and faces are clear before you upload.
