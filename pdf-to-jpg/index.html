How to convert a PDF to JPG

  1. Choose your PDF file.
  2. Choose the pages you want (or leave it empty for all) and the image quality. Standard (150 dpi) suits most uses; High is sharper but larger.
  3. Press Convert to JPG. Each page becomes a JPG image you can download one by one or all together as a ZIP.

The PDF is opened and drawn by your browser, so the file is never sent to a server. That keeps documents such as certificates and bank statements private.

Large PDFs. Very long or heavy PDFs can be slow on older phones. If the page stops, try the Small quality option or split the PDF first. If a PDF has a password, the tool asks for it.

नेपालीमा: PDF लाई फोटो (JPG) मा कसरी बदल्ने?

  1. PDF फाइल छान्नुहोस् वा तानेर राख्नुहोस्।
  2. फोटोको गुणस्तर (Small, Standard वा High) र फर्म्याट (JPG वा PNG) छान्नुहोस्।
  3. सबै पेज चाहिँदैन भने "Pages" मा जस्तै 1-3, 5 लेख्नुहोस्; खाली छोड्दा सबै पेज बन्छन्।
  4. "Convert" थिच्नुहोस्, त्यसपछि पेज एक-एक गरी वा "Download all (ZIP)" बाट सबै डाउनलोड गर्नुहोस्।

पासवर्ड भएको PDF भए टुलले पासवर्ड सोध्छ। तपाईंको PDF कुनै सर्भरमा पठाइँदैन, सबै काम ब्राउजरमै हुन्छ। धेरै ठूला PDF पुराना मोबाइलमा ढिलो हुन सक्छन्।

PDF का केही पेज मात्र कसरी बदल्ने?

"Pages" मा चाहिएको पेज नम्बर लेख्नुहोस्, जस्तै 2-4 वा 1, 3।

खुल्छ, तर पासवर्ड थाहा हुनुपर्छ। टुलले सोध्छ र त्यसपछि पेज बदल्छ।

धेरैजसो कामका लागि Standard पुग्छ। सानो अक्षर ठूलो गरेर हेर्नुपर्‍यो भने High छान्नुहोस्।

Frequently asked questions

Is this PDF to JPG converter free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and adds no watermark.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is read and converted in your browser, so it stays on your device.

Does it convert every page?

Yes. Each page of the PDF becomes its own JPG image, named with the page number. The tool stops at 100 pages to keep your browser responsive.

Which quality should I choose?

Standard (150 dpi) is good for screens and most forms. Choose High (220 dpi) if you need to zoom into small print, or Small to keep files light.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, on current Android and iPhone browsers. Large PDFs may be slow on older phones.

Can it open a password-protected PDF?

Yes, if you know the password. The tool asks for it and then converts the pages.
