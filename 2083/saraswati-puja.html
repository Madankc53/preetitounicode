Saraswati Puja 2083 date

Date: Thursday, February 11, 2027, which is Magh 28, 2083 BS. It is observed on Magh Shukla Panchami, the fifth day of the bright lunar fortnight. The festival is also called Shree Panchami and Basanta Panchami, and it marks the start of spring.

What happens on Saraswati Puja?

Saraswati is the goddess of knowledge, music and learning. Schools, colleges and families set up her image, and students place books, pens and instruments in front of her for blessing. Many children are introduced to writing on this day (their first letters). People wear yellow, and the day is considered auspicious for starting studies.
