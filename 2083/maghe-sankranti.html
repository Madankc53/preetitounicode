Maghe Sankranti 2083 date

Date: Friday, January 15, 2027, which is Magh 1, 2083 BS. Maghe Sankranti always falls on the first day of Magh, when the sun enters Capricorn (Makar), so the English date is usually January 14 or 15.

What is Maghe Sankranti?

Maghe Sankranti (also Makar Sankranti) marks the end of the cold month of Poush and the start of longer, warmer days. People bathe at rivers and holy confluences, such as Devghat, Triveni and Sankhu, and visit temples. The festival food includes ghee, chaku (molasses), til laddu (sesame sweets), yam (tarul), sweet potato and spinach. Elders give blessings and families share a meal together.

For the Tharu community it is Maghi, their New Year, with dance and community feasts, especially in the Terai. The Magar community also celebrates it as a major festival.
