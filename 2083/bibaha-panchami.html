Bibaha Panchami 2083 date

Date: Monday, December 14, 2026, which is Mangsir 28, 2083 BS. Bibaha Panchami (also Vivah Panchami) falls on Mangsir Shukla Panchami, the fifth day of the bright lunar fortnight in the month of Mangsir (Margashirsha).

What it marks: the wedding of Lord Ram and Sita in Janakpur. The main celebration is at Janaki Mandir in Janakpurdham, Madhesh Province, where a wedding procession and a ceremonial marriage are held. It is listed as a holiday in Madhesh Province.

In Mithila tradition many families avoid holding their own weddings on this day. Mangsir 28 is also not among the official marriage sait dates for 2083 (listed below). Ask your pandit if you are planning a ceremony near it.
