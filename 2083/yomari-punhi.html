Yomari Punhi 2083 date

Date: Thursday, December 24, 2026, which is Poush 9, 2083 BS. It falls on the full moon (Purnima) after the rice harvest. The 2083 holiday list names this day as Dhanya Purnima, and the Newar community celebrates the same full moon as Yomari Punhi.

What is Yomari Punhi?

Yomari Punhi is a Newar harvest festival. Families make yomari, a steamed dumpling of rice-flour dough shaped like a fig, filled with chaku (hardened molasses) and sesame, or sometimes khuwa. The name comes from Newar words meaning "tasty bread" and "full moon". Yomari is offered to the gods first, then shared with relatives and neighbours. The festival is especially lively in Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Bhaktapur and Panauti.

Dhanya Purnima, "grain full moon", is the wider Hindu harvest full moon, when families give thanks for the new rice.
