What is Tamu Lhosar?

Tamu Lhosar is the New Year of the Gurung community, who call themselves Tamu. "Lho" means year and "sar" means new. People wear traditional dress, hold cultural programmes with music and dance, and gather with family. Celebrations are largest in Pokhara and Kaski, Lamjung, Gorkha, Tanahun, Syangja and Kathmandu, where Gurung communities live.

Nepal has three main Lhosar festivals: Tamu Lhosar (Gurung, Poush 15), Sonam Lhosar (Tamang and Sherpa communities, Magh 24 in 2083, Sunday Feb 7, 2027) and Gyalpo Lhosar (Falgun).
