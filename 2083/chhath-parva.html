Chhath 2083 dates and arghya times

Chhath is a four-day festival of sun worship. In 2083 the main day, with the evening offering to the setting sun (Sandhya Arghya), is Sunday, November 15, 2026 (Kartik 29), on Kartik Shukla Shashthi.

What is Chhath?

Chhath Parva is dedicated to the sun god Surya and Chhathi Maiya. It is celebrated mostly by Madhesi and Tharu communities and by Bihari and Maithili families, with large gatherings on river banks, ponds and ghats across Nepal's Terai and in Kathmandu. Devotees fast without water for about 36 hours and offer fruit, thekua and sugarcane to the sun.

What time is the Chhath arghya in Kathmandu?

In Kathmandu the sun sets at about 5:11 PM on November 15 (Sandhya Arghya) and rises at about 6:25 AM on November 16 (Usha Arghya). Times differ slightly by city.

What are the four days of Chhath?

Nahay-Khay (Nov 13), Kharna (Nov 14), Sandhya Arghya (Nov 15) and Usha Arghya (Nov 16).
