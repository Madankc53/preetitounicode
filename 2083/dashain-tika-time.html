Dashain 2083 key dates

Dashain 2083 runs from Sunday 11 October to Sunday 25 October 2026.

  • GhatasthapanaSunday, 11 October 2026 · Ashwin 25 · Jamara is planted
  • FulpatiSaturday, 17 October 2026
  • Maha AshtamiSunday, 18 October 2026 · Kartik 1
  • Maha NavamiTuesday, 20 October 2026 · Kartik 3
  • Vijaya Dashami (main tika day)Wednesday, 21 October 2026 · Kartik 4 · Elders give tika and jamara
  • Ekadashi and DwadashiThursday 22 and Friday 23 October 2026 · Kartik 5 and 6 · Tika continues
  • Kojagrat PurnimaSunday, 25 October 2026 · Kartik 8 · Dashain ends

How to find your correct tika time abroad

  1. Pick your city in the table above. Use the nearest listed city in the same time zone if yours is missing, but wait for the official list before fixing the time.

Frequently asked questions

When is Dashain Tika in 2083 BS?

Vijaya Dashami, the main tika day, falls on Wednesday, 21 October 2026, which is Kartik 4, 2083 BS.

What is the Dashain tika time in the USA, UK or Australia?

The official city-wise tika times for 2083 have not been published yet. They are normally released by the International Calendar Decision Committee before the festival, and this page will list them once they are announced. Times differ by city and can fall on a different date than in Nepal.

Can I convert the Nepal tika time to my local time?

Not reliably. Official lists give a separate time for each city, and in past years some countries had a different date from Nepal. Use the official city-wise list. The time difference column on this page only shows how far the clocks are apart.

When does Dashain 2083 start and end?

Dashain 2083 begins with Ghatasthapana on Sunday, 11 October 2026 (Ashwin 25) and ends with Kojagrat Purnima on Sunday, 25 October 2026 (Kartik 8).

Who announces the Dashain tika time?

The Nepal Panchanga Nirnayak Samiti announces the tika time for Nepal. Country-wise times for Nepalis abroad have been published by the International Calendar Decision Committee in past years.
