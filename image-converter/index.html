    How to convert an image to JPG

    1. Choose your images (PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP or JPG).
    2. Select the format you want, then the quality.
    3. Press Convert and download each file, or all of them in a ZIP.

    Many forms and websites accept only JPG. WebP photos saved from a phone or the web often need converting first. Converting to JPG replaces any transparent area with white.

    Which format should I use?

    • JPG: best for photos and the most widely accepted.
    • PNG: best for logos, screenshots and images with transparency; files are larger.
    • WebP: smaller files on websites; not accepted everywhere.

    Need a particular file size? Use the image compressor. To make a PDF, use JPG to PDF.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I convert PNG to JPG?

    Choose the PNG, select JPG as the output format and press Convert. Transparent areas become white.

    How do I convert WebP to JPG?

    Choose the WebP file, select JPG and press Convert. It works the same way as any other image.

    Is this image converter free and private?

    Yes. It is free and the files are converted in your browser, so they are never uploaded.

    Will converting reduce the quality?

    Converting to JPG or WebP is lossy, so choose High quality if detail matters. Converting to PNG keeps every pixel but makes larger files.

    Can I convert many images at once?

    Yes. Select several and download them individually or as a ZIP.

    Does it support JPG to PNG?

    Yes. Choose JPG files and select PNG as the output format.
