How to convert Mangal to Preeti

  1. Paste your Mangal or any Unicode Nepali text into the left box (or press Paste).
  2. The Preeti text appears on the right instantly. Press Copy.
  3. In Word, PageMaker or InDesign, paste the text and change its font to Preeti. The Nepali letters appear.

Mangal, Unicode and Preeti: what is the difference?

Mangal is the default Nepali font in Windows. It is a Unicode font, so text typed in Mangal can be searched, copied between apps and shown on websites.

Preeti is a legacy font. It stores Nepali as ordinary English keystrokes and only looks Nepali because the font draws different shapes for those keys. Many printing presses, government offices and older designs still require Preeti. This tool bridges the two.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mangal the same as Unicode?

Mangal is a font; the text you type with it is Unicode. Any Unicode Nepali text (Mangal, Kalimati, Noto Sans Devanagari and others) converts the same way.

Why does the Preeti output look like English?

Preeti text is English keystrokes. It shows Nepali only when the Preeti font is applied to it.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion runs in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.

Can I convert Preeti back to Unicode?

Yes, use the Preeti to Unicode converter.
