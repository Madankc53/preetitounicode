What this site does and does not do with your information, in plain language.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

The short version

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and the tools do not have forms that collect your name or email.
  • The text you type or upload into the font converters is processed in your browser. It is not sent to our servers.
  • The site shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show and measure ads.
  • We do not add analytics or tracking scripts of our own, and we do not set our own cookies.

What stays in your browser

The tools remember a few preferences on your device using your browser's local storage, so the page looks the way you left it. These are: light or dark theme, language choice, and the output font size of the converters. This data never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Converting text, files and dates happens on your device. Some tools offer a share link. On the Kantipur converter the shared text is placed after the # in the link. Browsers do not send that part to a server, but anyone who has the link can read the text, so do not share links containing private text.

Third parties that may receive your data

Like most websites, this one loads some resources from other companies. When your browser requests them, those companies can see your IP address and basic browser details.

We do not set cookies of our own. Cookies may be set by the advertising services above. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them may change which ads you see, but the tools will keep working.
