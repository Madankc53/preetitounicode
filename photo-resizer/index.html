How to resize a passport photo or signature

  1. Add your photo or signature image, or take one with your camera.
  2. Pick a common size, or type the exact width and height in pixels or millimetres, and the maximum KB.
  3. Drag the picture into place, zoom if needed, then download. The file size shows live before you download.

For a signature, sign on plain white paper, photograph it in good light, then choose Show whole image so nothing is cropped. For a face photo, choose Fill the frame, switch on the face guide and centre the face.

Follow your form's instructions. The sizes listed here are common examples, not official requirements. Each form, exam body or office sets its own pixel size, KB limit and background colour, so check its notice and enter those values. Check the result before you upload it.

Pixels, millimetres and KB

Pixels set how large the image is on screen, and KB is the file size. A 35 × 45 mm photo at 300 dpi is about 413 × 531 pixels. Switch the unit to millimetres and set the dpi if your form gives a size in mm. A smaller KB limit lowers the JPEG quality, so choose the largest limit your form allows for the sharpest result.

Frequently asked questions

How do I resize a photo to a specific size in pixels?

Add the photo, type the width and height in pixels, adjust the position and download. The file has exactly that size.

How do I make a photo under 50 KB or 100 KB?

Set the maximum file size to 50 KB or 100 KB. The tool lowers the quality until the file fits and shows the final size before you download.

Can I enter the size in millimetres?

Yes. Change the unit to millimetres, enter the width and height, and set the resolution (300 dpi is common). The tool shows the pixel size it will produce.

Can I use it for a signature?

Yes. Choose a signature size, use Show whole image so nothing is cropped, and set the KB limit your form asks for.

Are these the official sizes for Nepali forms?

No. They are common examples. Always use the exact size, KB limit and background colour written on your form or notice.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser, so the image stays on your device.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Take a photo or choose one from your gallery, then drag with your finger to position it.
