How to resize a passport photo or signature

  1. Choose your photo or signature image.
  2. Pick a common size or type the exact width and height in pixels, and set the maximum file size in KB.
  3. Drag the picture into position, zoom if needed, then press Create image and download the JPG.

For a signature, photograph or scan it on plain white paper, then use the Show whole image fit so nothing is cropped. For a face photo, use Fill the frame and drag until the face is centred.

Follow your form's instructions. The sizes listed here are common examples, not official requirements. Every form, exam body or office sets its own pixel size, KB limit and background colour, so check its notice and type those values in. Check the result before you upload it.

Pixels, mm and KB

Pixels set how large the image is on screen, and KB is the file size. A 35 x 45 mm photo at 300 dpi is about 413 x 531 pixels. A smaller KB limit lowers the JPEG quality, so choose the largest limit your form allows for the sharpest result.

Frequently asked questions

How do I resize a photo to a specific size in pixels?

Choose the photo, type the width and height in pixels, adjust the position and press Create image. The download has exactly that size.

How do I reduce a photo to under 50 KB or 100 KB?

Set the Max file size to 50 KB or 100 KB. The tool lowers the quality until the file fits and shows the final size.

Can I use it for a signature?

Yes. Choose a signature size such as 300 x 100 px, use Show whole image so nothing is cropped, and set the KB limit your form asks for.

Are these the official sizes for Nepali forms?

No. They are common examples. Always use the exact size and KB limit written on your form or notice.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is done in your browser, so the image stays on your device.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Choose a photo from your gallery or camera, then drag with your finger to position it.
