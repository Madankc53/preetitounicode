The five days of Tihar

Tihar honours crows (Kaag), dogs (Kukur), cows and the goddess Laxmi, then ox and Govardhan, and ends with Bhai Tika, when sisters put a seven-coloured tika on their brothers. The fourth day is also the New Year of the Newar community (Nepal Sambat) and Mha Puja.

When will the official date be announced?

Official dates are normally published late in the previous year (the 2083 holiday list was published in Falgun 2082), so check back around Falgun-Chaitra 2083 (Feb-Apr 2027). For comparison, see Tihar 2083 and Bhai Tika 2083.
