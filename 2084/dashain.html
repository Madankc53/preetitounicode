Why is Dashain earlier in 2084?

In 2083 Dashain ran from Oct 11 to Oct 21, 2026. In 2084 it starts about 11 days earlier, because the lunar year and the solar BS year drift apart until an extra month (Mala Maas) is added. 2083 had one; 2084 is a regular 12-month year.

When will the official date be announced?

Official dates are normally published late in the previous year (the 2083 holiday list was published in Falgun 2082), so check back around Falgun-Chaitra 2083 (Feb-Apr 2027). Until then, plan with the dates above but avoid booking non-refundable travel on a single day. For comparison, see the official Dashain 2083 dates.
