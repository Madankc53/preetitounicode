Frequently asked questions

When did Nepali year 2082 start?

Baisakh 1, 2082 BS was Monday, 14 April 2025. The year ended on Chaitra 30, 2082, which was Monday, 13 April 2026. Baisakh 1, 2083 followed on 14 April 2026.

How many days are in each month of 2082 BS?

Baisakh 31, Jestha 31, Ashadh 32, Shrawan 31, Bhadra 31, Ashwin 31, Kartik 30, Mangsir 29, Poush 30, Magh 29, Falgun 30 and Chaitra 30, a total of 365 days.

What is Dashain 2082 in AD?

The government holiday list for 2082 gives Ghatasthapana on Ashwin 6 (22 September 2025) and the Dashain holidays from Ashwin 13 to Ashwin 18 (29 September to 4 October 2025).

Is the 2082 calendar the same as the 2083 one?

The two years happen to have the same month lengths, but they start on different weekdays, so the weekday of each date is different.
