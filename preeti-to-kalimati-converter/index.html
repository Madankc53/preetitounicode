How to convert Preeti to Kalimati

  1. Paste your Preeti text (the English-looking letters such as g]kfn) into the left box.
  2. Press Copy to copy the Nepali Unicode result.
  3. Paste it into Word, Google Docs or a website and choose the Kalimati font. Because the text is Unicode, it works with Kalimati, Mangal, Noto Sans Devanagari and other fonts.

Preeti vs Kalimati

Preeti is a legacy font that stores Nepali as English keystrokes. Kalimati is a Unicode font used by Nepali government offices and publications. There is no direct key-to-key mapping from Preeti to Kalimati; the correct way is Preeti → Unicode → Kalimati, which is exactly what this converter does.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kalimati a Unicode font?

Yes. Kalimati is a Unicode Nepali font, so the converted text can also be shown in any other Unicode Nepali font.

Why is there no Kalimati-specific output?

Unicode text is independent of the font. Choose Kalimati in your editor and the same text is displayed in it.

Where can I get the Kalimati font?

From the original font publisher or your office. This site does not host it.

Can I convert a Word file?

Use the Preeti to Unicode converter, which supports .docx and .txt uploads.
