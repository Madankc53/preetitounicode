How to sign a PDF online

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Draw your signature with your finger or mouse, or upload a photo of your signature on white paper, then press Use signature.
  3. Open the page you want to sign and press Place signature on this page. Drag the signature into position and drag the red dot to resize it. Repeat on other pages if needed.
  4. Press Sign PDF and download the signed file.

The PDF and your signature are handled in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and no account is needed.

What kind of signature is this?

The tool places an image of your handwritten signature on the page, like signing a printout and scanning it. It is not a digital certificate signature. If an office, bank or court asks for a specific type of electronic signature, follow its instructions.

Tips. Sign on a phone for the most natural result. For a photo of a paper signature, use a dark pen on white paper in good light. If the file is too large to upload afterwards, use Compress PDF. To keep only the pages you need, use Split PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is this Sign PDF tool free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and adds no watermark.

Is my PDF or signature uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is opened and signed in your browser, and your signature is not stored or sent anywhere.

Can I sign a PDF on my phone?

Yes. Draw your signature with your finger, place it on the page and download the file.

Can I sign more than one page?

Yes. Go to each page and press Place signature on this page. Pages without a signature stay unchanged.

Is this a legally binding digital signature?

It adds an image of your signature. Whether that is accepted depends on the office or form, so follow the instructions you were given.

Why can I not sign some pages?

Pages that are rotated in the PDF are not supported yet. Choose another page, or rotate the file before signing.

Does it work with password-protected PDFs?

No. Remove the password first, then add the file.
