How to split a PDF or take pages out

  1. Choose your PDF. The tool shows how many pages it has.
  2. Pick what you want: keep only some pages, remove some pages, or save every page as its own PDF.
  3. Type the page numbers, for example 1-3, 5, then press Split PDF and download the result.

Your original file is not changed. A new file is made on your device, and nothing is uploaded.

Common uses

  • Keep only the pages a form asks for, such as the first two pages of a long scan.
  • Remove a blank or wrong page before you upload a document.
  • Make a file smaller by sending only the pages that are needed.

Page numbers. Page numbers count from the first page of the file, as shown in your PDF viewer. You can type a range such as 2-4 and single pages such as 7, separated by commas. Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first.

To join files instead, use Merge PDF. To turn pages into pictures, use PDF to JPG.

नेपालीमा: PDF का पेज कसरी छुट्याउने वा हटाउने?

  1. PDF फाइल छान्नुहोस्।
  2. के गर्ने छान्नुहोस्: चाहिएका पेज मात्र राख्ने, केही पेज हटाउने, वा हरेक पेज अलग PDF बनाउने।
  3. "Pages" मा पेज नम्बर लेख्नुहोस्, जस्तै 1-3, 5।
  4. "Split PDF" थिचेर बनेको फाइल डाउनलोड गर्नुहोस्।

PDF तपाईंको डिभाइसबाट बाहिर जाँदैन, सबै काम ब्राउजरमै हुन्छ। पासवर्ड भएको PDF खोल्न सकिँदैन, पहिले पासवर्ड हटाउनुहोस्। मूल फाइल जस्ताको तस्तै रहन्छ, नयाँ फाइल मात्र बन्छ।

PDF बाट एउटा मात्र पेज कसरी निकाल्ने?

"Keep only these pages" छानेर "Pages" मा त्यो पेजको नम्बर लेख्नुहोस्, जस्तै 4। त्यसपछि "Split PDF" थिच्नुहोस्।

PDF बाट पेज कसरी हटाउने?

"Remove these pages" छानेर हटाउनुपर्ने पेज नम्बर लेख्नुहोस्, जस्तै 2, 5-6। बाँकी पेजको नयाँ PDF बन्छ।

मूल PDF बिग्रन्छ?

बिग्रँदैन। मूल फाइल जस्ताको तस्तै रहन्छ र नयाँ फाइल मात्र बनिन्छ।

Frequently asked questions

Is this Split PDF tool free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and adds no watermark.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The file is processed in your browser on your own device.

How do I extract one page from a PDF?

Choose Keep only these pages, type the page number, such as 4, and press Split PDF.

How do I delete a page from a PDF?

Choose Remove these pages, type the page numbers you do not want, such as 2, 5-6, and press Split PDF. You get a new PDF without those pages.

Can I save each page as a separate PDF?

Yes. Choose Save every page separately. You get a ZIP file with one PDF per page, up to 300 pages.

Does it work with password-protected PDFs?

No. Remove the password first, then add the file.
